# Support & Community Channels

Welcome to the **GLASTOR® Core** community! We provide several channels to help you build,
integrate, and scale your applications with confidence.

---

## 💬 Community Channels (Free & Open)

For questions, architecture discussions, or sharing your projects:

- **[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/glastor-core/discussions)** _(Recommended)_:
  - **Q&A**: Ask implementation and integration questions. Get answers voted by the community and
    maintainers.
  - **Ideas & RFCs**: Propose new features, design patterns, or major enhancements.
  - **Announcements**: Official release notes, roadmap updates, and ecosystem news.
- **[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/glastor-core/glastor-core/issues)**:
  - Use **Issues** exclusively for reproducible bug reports and tracked feature requests using our
    [YAML Forms](https://github.com/glastor-core/glastor-core/tree/main/.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE).
  - Please do not use GitHub Issues for general troubleshooting questions; use Discussions instead.

---

## 🛡️ Security Vulnerabilities

**Do NOT report security vulnerabilities via public issues or discussions.**

Please follow our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md):

- Submit a private advisory via
  **[GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting](https://github.com/glastor-core/glastor-core/security/advisories/new)**.
- Contact the security maintainers directly at `security@glastor.dev`.

---

## 🏢 Enterprise & Commercial Support

Organizations running mission-critical workloads can request enterprise-grade support:

- Dedicated response SLAs (Sev-1 / Sev-2 priority escalation).
- Architecture reviews, performance profiling, and capacity planning.
- Custom module development and LTS security backports.

Inquiries: `support@glastor.dev`

---

## ⏱️ Response Time Expectations

- **Security Advisories**: Initial response within 24–48 hours.
- **Bug Reports (P0/Breaking)**: Triaged within 48–72 hours.
- **Community Discussions & Questions**: Best-effort basis by maintainers and community
  contributors.
